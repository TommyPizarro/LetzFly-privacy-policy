Privacy Policy for LetzFly

Last updated: November 2, 2026

LetzFly ("we", "our", or "app") is a travel planning application designed to help users organize trips, itineraries, budgets, and packing lists. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your first name, last name, and credentials locally on your device to secure your login session.
  • User-Generated Content: Travel itineraries, budgets, bucket lists, and notes created within the app are stored locally on your device.

2. Data Storage and Security

All personal data, account preferences, and travel records are stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage mechanisms and Room Database. We do not operate external servers, and your data is never transmitted to third parties.

3. Data Deletion and Account Removal

You have full control over your data. If you choose to delete your account via the app's Profile screen, all associated local database records, preferences, and cached details are permanently wiped from your device.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via email at letzflysupport@gmail.com.
